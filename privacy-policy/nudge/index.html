Privacy Policy - Nudge

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Nudge collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.

Nudge is an Android screen-time tool that runs entirely on your device.

What Nudge can see

To know when to interrupt you, Nudge uses Android's accessibility service to observe:

  • which app is currently in the foreground, and
  • the address bar of supported web browsers.

This information is checked against the blocklist you configured, in memory, and then discarded. It is never written to storage, never sent anywhere, and never shared with anyone. Nudge does not read page content, messages, form fields, passwords, or anything you type.

Nudge also uses Android's usage-access permission to show you your own screen-time statistics. That data stays on the device.

What Nudge stores on your device

Kept in the app's private storage, readable only by Nudge:

  • Your settings - timer lengths, session limit, strict mode
  • Your blocklist - the apps, website categories and domains you chose
  • Your history - how many times you were interrupted, by what, and for how long, kept for a maximum of 35 days and then deleted automatically

Uninstalling Nudge, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes all of it permanently. There is no backup and no copy anywhere else.

Purchases

Nudge Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google - Nudge never sees your card details, billing address, or Google account. The app stores only a single on/off flag recording that a purchase was made.

Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Nudge is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Third parties

Nudge contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. The only third party involved is Google Play, and only when you make a purchase.

Changes

If a future version of Nudge ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here and in the Play Store listing's Data Safety section.
